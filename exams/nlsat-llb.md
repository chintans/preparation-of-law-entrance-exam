# NLSAT-LLB 2027 — NLSIU Bengaluru, 3-year LLB (Hons)

- **Leads to:** 3-year LLB (Hons) at NLSIU. Same test date also covers BA (Hons), MPP and PhD.
- **Eligibility:** Bachelor's degree in any discipline, 45% aggregate (40% SC/ST/OBC-NCL/PwD).
- **Exam:** 25 Apr 2027 (Sun), offline. Application expected Nov 2026, closes 23 Mar 2027.
  2026 cycle: exam 26 Apr 2026, 10:00–12:30.
- **Fee:** ₹3,500 (General).

## Pattern — 150 minutes, 150 marks

### Part A — objective, 75 marks
75 MCQs, three sections of 25 (per coaching sources — confirm in notification):

| Section | Qs | Topic IDs |
|---|---|---|
| English comprehension (~500-word passages) | 25 | ENG-RC |
| Current affairs & GK | 25 | GK-CA, GK-POL, LEG-NEW |
| Critical/logical reasoning incl. basic legal aptitude | 25 | LR-CR, LR-ARG, LEG-PRIN |

Marking: +1 correct, **−0.25 for wrong *or unanswered*** — skipping is not free here, unlike CLAT/AILET.

### Part B — descriptive, 75 marks
- Legal-scenario short answers (no prior law needed) — ~60 marks. Sources conflict on count
  (5 × 12 marks vs 10 short questions) and word limits (150–300 words). **Confirm in notification.**
- One essay, up to 500 words, from a choice of three contemporary topics — ~15 marks.
- Part B is marked only for candidates shortlisted on Part A (1:5 ratio). Tie-break: higher Part B.

Topic IDs: WRIT-LEGAL, WRIT-ESSAY.

## Selection
Combined Part A + Part B. Cut-off above 75th percentile (General/EWS), 40th (reserved categories).
