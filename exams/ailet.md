# AILET 2027 (BA LLB)

- **Conducted by:** National Law University Delhi — nationallawuniversitydelhi.in
- **Leads to:** 5-year BA LLB (Hons) at NLU Delhi (not a 3-year LLB)
- **Eligibility:** Class 12 with 45% (40% SC/ST/PwD). No age limit.
- **Exam:** 13 Dec 2026, 2:00–4:00 PM, offline. Registration closes 10 Nov 2026. Admit card ~30 Nov.

## Pattern

150 MCQs · 120 minutes (raised from 90; one table on Careers360 still shows 90 — confirm on admit card) ·
+1 correct, −0.25 wrong, no penalty for unattempted.

| Section | Questions | Topic IDs |
|---|---|---|
| English Language | 50 | ENG-RC, ENG-VOC, ENG-GRM |
| Current Affairs & GK | 30 | GK-CA, GK-STA, GK-POL, LEG-NEW |
| Logical Reasoning (includes legal reasoning) | 70 | LR-CR, LR-ARG, LR-ANA, LEG-PRIN and law topics |

No quantitative section. Speed matters more than in CLAT: 150 questions in 120 minutes is
48 seconds a question.

## Differences from CLAT worth drilling

- More grammar and vocabulary (error spotting, fill-in-the-blank, synonyms/antonyms).
- Static GK appears alongside current affairs.
- Analytical puzzles (arrangement, blood relations, series, coding) sit inside the 70 reasoning questions.
