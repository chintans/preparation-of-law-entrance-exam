# LSAT India — discontinued

LSAC (USA), which ran LSAT India through Pearson VUE, stopped offering it. The last sessions
were January and May 2024; LSAC's notice cites a business decision. No sitting since.

**What former LSAT India colleges use now**

- **Jindal Global Law School:** LNAT-UK for the 5-year and 3-year LLB (Hons); CLAT PG for LLM.
- **UPES Dehradun:** its own ULSAT, CLAT-UG rank, Class 12 merit or CUET-UG (5-year LLB).
- Others (Alliance, BITS Law, VIT Law, etc.): own test or CLAT/CUET — check each.

**Plan impact:** removed from scheduling. If Chintan wants Jindal's 3-year LLB, add LNAT
(reading-based MCQs + essay — strongly overlaps NLSAT) as a tracked exam. Asked in setup summary.
