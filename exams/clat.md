# CLAT 2027 (UG)

- **Conducted by:** Consortium of National Law Universities — consortiumofnlus.ac.in
- **Leads to:** 5-year integrated LLB at participating NLUs (not a 3-year LLB)
- **Eligibility:** Class 12 with 45% (40% SC/ST). **No upper age limit, no attempt limit.**
- **Exam:** 6 Dec 2026, 2:00–4:00 PM, offline pen-and-paper. Registration closes 31 Oct 2026.
- **2027 pattern:** unchanged from 2026 (Governing Body decision, 18 Jul 2026). A proposed
  three-section format could apply from CLAT 2028 at the earliest.

## Pattern

120 MCQs · 120 minutes · +1 correct, −0.25 wrong · every question is passage-based.

| Section | Questions | Weight | Topic IDs |
|---|---|---|---|
| English Language | 22–26 | ~20% | ENG-RC, ENG-VOC |
| Current Affairs incl. GK | 28–32 | ~25% | GK-CA, GK-POL, GK-ECO, LEG-NEW |
| Legal Reasoning | 28–32 | ~25% | LEG-PRIN, LEG-TORT, LEG-CONT, LEG-CRIM, LEG-CONST, LEG-OTH |
| Logical Reasoning | 22–26 | ~20% | LR-CR, LR-ARG |
| Quantitative Techniques | 10–14 | ~10% | QT-DI, QT-ARITH |

## What the questions look like

- **English:** ~450-word passages from contemporary or historically significant prose; questions
  on main idea, inference, author's view, argument, vocabulary in context.
- **Current affairs:** passages built on news of roughly the past year; questions test knowledge
  around the passage, not just inside it. Legal/judicial developments feature heavily.
- **Legal reasoning:** passage states a rule or principle plus facts; apply the principle as
  stated, even if real law differs. Awareness of current legal issues helps.
- **Logical reasoning:** short arguments — identify conclusion and premises, assumptions,
  strengthen/weaken, inference, analogies, contradictions.
- **Quant:** short data sets, graphs or text with numbers; Class 10 level maths.
