# CUET PG 2027 — LLB paper (DU 3-year LLB and other universities)

- **Conducted by:** NTA — exams.nta.nic.in
- **Leads to:** 3-year LLB at Delhi University (~2,888 seats) and other participating universities.
- **DU eligibility:** graduation with 50% (45% OBC/PwD, 40% SC/ST).
- **Dates (tentative):** registration expected Dec 2026; exam window Mar 2027
  (1–5, 8, 12–20, 24–25 Mar; buffer 30–31 Mar). LLB paper day not yet announced.
- **Paper code:** DU LLB has used the general paper **COQP11**. Confirm in the 2027 bulletin.

## Pattern

75 MCQs · 90 minutes · computer-based · +4 correct, −1 wrong · 300 marks.
72 seconds a question; −1 per error means guess only after eliminating two options.

## Syllabus (general paper)

| Unit | Topics | Topic IDs |
|---|---|---|
| English comprehension | grammar, error spotting, sentence correction, para jumbles, RC, vocabulary, idioms | ENG-RC, ENG-GRM, ENG-VOC |
| Hindi comprehension | grammar, passage, synonyms/antonyms, idioms, one-word substitution | HIN (check in bulletin whether compulsory) |
| GK & awareness | history, Constitution, polity, economy, science, geography, culture, sports, legal awareness, current affairs | GK-STA, GK-POL, GK-ECO, GK-CA, LEG-NEW |
| Computer basics | fundamentals, abbreviations, shortcuts, MS Office, hardware/software, storage, I/O devices, internet, viruses | COMP |
| General aptitude | averages, percentages, ratio, P&L, interest, time & work, time & distance, LCM/HCF, mensuration | QT-ARITH, QT-GEO |
| Logical reasoning | ranking, arrangement, series, blood relations, coding-decoding, analogy, syllogism, statements & arguments, assumptions | LR-ANA, LR-ARG |

Section-wise question counts are not published.
