# Unified topic map

One list of topics across all exams. Each session teaches and tests topics by ID; mastery is
tracked per ID in `progress/state.json`.

Weight per exam: **3** = core/heavy, **2** = regular, **1** = occasional, **–** = not tested.

| ID | Topic | What to master | CLAT | AILET | CUET | NLSAT |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ENG-RC | Reading comprehension | main idea, inference, tone, author's view, argument structure; 450–500-word passages at speed | 3 | 3 | 2 | 3 |
| ENG-VOC | Vocabulary & idioms | meaning in context, synonyms/antonyms, idioms, one-word substitution | 1 | 2 | 2 | 1 |
| ENG-GRM | Grammar | error spotting, sentence correction, fill-ins, para jumbles | – | 2 | 2 | – |
| HIN | Hindi comprehension | passage, grammar, muhavare, synonyms | – | – | 1 | – |
| GK-CA | Current affairs | last ~12 months: national, international, economy, schemes, reports & indices, awards, sports, appointments, summits | 3 | 3 | 2 | 3 |
| LEG-NEW | Legal current affairs | landmark SC/HC judgments, new statutes (BNS, BNSS, BSA, DPDP Act etc.), legal institutions | 3 | 2 | 1 | 3 |
| GK-POL | Polity & Constitution | Preamble, FRs, DPSPs, Parliament, judiciary, federalism, amendments, constitutional bodies | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| GK-ECO | Economy basics | GDP, inflation, RBI & monetary policy, budget, trade, banking terms | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
